02 / The problem

Multi-channel intake

One job, reported four ways, is still one job.

Every platform in this category solves intake inside its own front door. None of them reconciles the same problem arriving by email, phone, SMS and portal into a single work order. So the coordinator does it from memory.

EmailFree text. No structure. Where the slowest jobs come from.
PhoneRetyped from a note. Detail lost between ear and keyboard.

PortalStructured — for the tenants who actually use it.

06 / Where we stop

Operational guardrails

Facilities teams aren’t fired for being slow.

They’re fired for a job that went wrong. So Readyset is built to prepare decisions, never to make them.

01

Your system stays the record

We write into it. We never become it. Turn us off tomorrow and you lose nothing.

02

Emergencies never touch us

Anything flagged urgent or safety-related goes straight to your existing human pathway, unprocessed.

03

Duplicates are suggested, never merged

Best-in-class detection is right about 63% of the time, and a wrong merge puts two tenants in one thread.

04

No trade or safety call is automated

We identify the asset and the access window. What’s wrong with it stays an authorised human judgement.

05

Every field shows its source

Tenant-stated, register-matched or coordinator-entered. The record stands up in a dispute.

06

Minimum tenant data

The smallest question that unblocks the job, in plain language, on the channel they already chose.
