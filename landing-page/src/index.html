The questions we actually get

Asked in the first ten minutes, every time.

01

Do we have to replace our work-order system?

No, and we’d argue against it. Your system stays the source of record — Readyset writes into it. There is no migration, no data to move, and switching us off tomorrow leaves you exactly where you started.

02

Isn’t this a feature our CMMS will ship next year?

Parts of it, probably. Structured intake already exists — ServiceChannel has the best in the category, and Re-Leased reads inbound email in Australia today. What none of them does is reconcile the same job arriving by email, phone, SMS and portal into one record. We checked ServiceChannel, Corrigo, Building Engines and Ecotrak: not one deduplicates at intake.

03

What happens if someone reports an emergency?

It never reaches us. Anything flagged urgent or safety-related is handed straight to the human pathway you already run, unprocessed and unqueued. We don’t triage it, delay it, or auto-reply to it.

04

How accurate is the duplicate detection?

Not accurate enough to act on by itself — the best published systems are right about 63% of the time. So Readyset suggests, and never merges. A wrong merge puts two tenants’ details in one thread, which is a privacy incident rather than an inconvenience. A person makes that call.

05

Do our tenants and contractors need to install anything?

Nothing. Emily keeps emailing the address she already emails. Marco keeps working out of simPRO. One forwarding rule on your maintenance mailbox is the entire integration — which matters, because every portal you’ve asked a contractor to adopt is a portal they haven’t.

06

What if it extracts something wrong?

Every field carries its source — tenant-stated, register-matched or coordinator-entered — so a wrong value is visible rather than buried. Nothing dispatches without a human pressing assign. Readyset prepares the record; it never approves it.

07

What do you do with our tenants’ data?

We ask the smallest question that unblocks the job and keep the least we can. For the inbox audit we read it, report on it, and delete it — nothing is retained without a signed agreement.

08

Where are you actually up to?

Early, and the next section says exactly which parts are real and which are staged. The fastest way to find out whether this works on your portfolio is the inbox audit — one week of messages, no integration, and you get the answer whether or not you buy anything.
