# Readyset — company summary & reel brief

*For the promo reel. Voice: precise, unhurried, credible. Never hyped — the restraint IS the brand.*

## The company in one line

**Readyset turns vague maintenance reports into dispatch-ready work orders — reported to ready in 4 minutes, not 2.4 days.**

## The company in one paragraph

Readyset is B2B software for commercial property facilities teams. Maintenance requests arrive incomplete and across four channels — email, phone, SMS, tenant portal — so the same job gets reported twice and a coordinator spends 2.4 days chasing a room number, an access window and a site contact before a contractor can be dispatched. Readyset sits in front of the work-order system the team already uses. Its AI reads every request, extracts the fields that stall a job, asks the requester one clarifying question, flags likely duplicates without merging them, and hands a human a dispatch-ready work order. The human approves; Readyset did the chasing.

## The numbers that carry the story

- **2.4 days → 4 minutes** — Time-to-Ready, the hero metric. Everything else supports this.
- **38%** of requests arrive needing clarification.
- **17%** duplicate an issue already reported.
- **1 question** — the agent asks the requester once, in plain language. No forms, no portals, no new app.

## Who it's for

Portfolio facilities managers running 5–50 mixed-use commercial buildings — and the coordinators who live in the intake queue. Tenants and contractors adopt nothing: they keep using email and phone.

## What makes it credible (say these, they're the differentiators)

1. **It joins your stack, it doesn't replace it.** Your work-order system stays the source of record.
2. **The AI prepares, a human decides.** Duplicate merges are suggested, never automatic. Nothing is written to your system without a human's click.
3. **Emergencies go straight to people.** The agent spots hazards fast and hands them off immediately — it never manages them.
4. **Every value shows its source.** Extracted with confidence scores, confirmed from replies. Trust by traceability.

## Brand kit for the edit

- **Palette:** Ink `#0B211B` · Spruce `#14382E` · Field `#5E7A6D` · Paper `#F5F2E9`. **Signal `#2FD284` only when something is ready** — the READY tag, the 4-minute metric, the final CTA. No pure white, no gradients, no drop shadows.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk (headlines, lowercase wordmark "readyset") · Inter (body) · IBM Plex Mono (IDs, timestamps, labels like SITE / ZONE / ACCESS).
- **Motion:** calm and exact — 150–250ms cuts, no bounce, no confetti. The pace itself should feel like the product: unhurried, checked twice.
- **Music:** minimal, steady, low-key confident. Nothing epic.
- **Never:** "Supercharge your ops with AI!", handshake stock footage, hard-hat clichés, saturated corporate blue.
