      How it works

      Readyset sits in front of the work-order system you already run. It owns the window from “request arrives” to “request is assignable” — everything after stays where it is today.

      1. Capture

        Ingests the channels that already exist — email, phone, SMS, portal. No new form, no new app for tenants or contractors.

      2. Clarify

        Extracts the fields that stall a job, then asks the requester one plain-language question for whatever's missing. The reply flows straight back into the record.

      3. Deduplicate

        Checks every open order and flags likely duplicates with its reasoning. It suggests the link — your coordinator confirms it.

      4. Ready

        Hands over a dispatch-ready work order: site, zone, asset, access window, contact. One click writes it to your existing system.

      Where a human always decides

      • Emergencies. Anything resembling gas, fire, flooding or electrical hazard is handed to a person immediately. The agent spots them fast; it never manages them.
      • Duplicate merges. The agent flags, the coordinator links. No silent merges.
      • The system of record. Every write to your work-order platform is a human approval click.
      • Dispatch and scope. Licensed trade decisions stay with licensed people.
